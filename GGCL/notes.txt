[1] GGCL-HOUSTON (BASE)
=======================
SOURCE: GGCL-master -> Adapted for Houston-2013 Few-Shot
FILES:
  - dataloader.py: Loads houston.mat (144 bands, 15 classes). Zero-padding patches.
  - run_kaggle.py: Auto-runs 1/5/10-shot experiments.
  - trainer.py: Contrastive + CE Loss. Weight ramp-up enabled.
PARAMS:
  - Patch: 13 | PCA: None (All 144 bands)
  - Norm: MinMax [0,1]
  - Split: Predefined Train/Test masks
PROTOCOL:
  - Train: n-shot/class from Train Mask
  - Unlabeled: Remainder of Train Mask
  - Test: 100% of Test Mask
KEY FEATS:
  - No Sample Loss (Zero-padding)
  - drop_last=False (For 1-shot stability)
